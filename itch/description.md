# Long Odds

**Plant a field. Bet a century.**

You are a god with one small valley and six families in it.

Each visit you can do three things:

- Build: clear fields, raise a seed store, plan a bridge.
- Teach: show people an idea. It lives only as long as someone remembers it.
- Set customs: how they farm, store, hunt and cut wood while you are gone.

Then you step out of the world for **1, 10, 100 or 1,000 years**. Each jump plays out in thirty seconds and you cannot touch anything until it lands.

When you come back, the village has grown, shrunk, or gone. A short report says why.

## What can go wrong

- The river floods.
- Heavy rain brings landslides.
- Tornadoes cross the plain.
- The volcano behind the hills sometimes erupts.
- People forget what you taught them.
- The forest and its game run out.

Disasters are rare. Over a thousand years, rare things happen.

## What you are aiming for

Keep the valley alive as long as you can. A village that lasts grows from hamlet to village, town and city, and finally into the Hold: one great house on the ridge for everyone.

The game never tells you what works. You find out by losing.

## How it plays

- A short tutorial comes first. It does not end well.
- Take as long as you like on each visit.
- Every valley is different. Enter a seed on the menu to replay one.
- No saves: a run lasts until nobody is left.
